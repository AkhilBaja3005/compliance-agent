# Scoped applicability assessment · 2026-11-14

This is an authored analysis of the intended commercial facts in [product intended use](../docs/product-intended-use.md), not a determination that the deterministic fake is a validated detector or a legally certified product. The [baseline](legal-baseline.json) pins Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 consolidated 2026-07-27, incorporating Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. EUR-Lex's exact requested pages returned an anti-bot response. The Commission service desk explicitly identifies the same consolidation. Full authoritative-text retrieval remains an external verification limit; the original regulation has not been substituted.

Territorial and operator assumptions: the fictional vendor is established in the EU and supplies its branded classifier system to EU editorial businesses from 2026-09-01. The provider/deployer assessment therefore assumes Union market and use connections; customers deploying it have their own context-dependent responsibilities. The vendor integrates a specialised classifier rather than training or distributing a model with general-purpose capability. Under these facts, it is not a GPAI-model provider. Upstream provider duties and contractual/data protection arrangements need separate review.

The [Article 6](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-6) routes do not match this purpose: it is neither an Annex I safety product/component nor a system intended for [Annex III](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/annex-3) uses. It reviews business drafts, not admission/learning outcomes/test misconduct, recruitment or worker performance. It has no biometrics, infrastructure safety, essential-service eligibility, policing, migration, justice or electoral influence purpose. This conclusion rests on functions, marketed purpose and deployment context, not the word “advisory,” and does not invoke the Article 6(3) derogation. Purpose validation supports this scope but cannot uncover disguised misuse. Expansion requires reassessment.

[Article 4 as amended](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-4) calls for context-sensitive measures supporting staff and relevant operators' AI literacy; it does not guarantee any individual's attainment. Role guidance, an accountable owner and versioned synthetic acknowledgments illustrate support. Their quality, actual participation and effectiveness cannot be proved from this repository. The access gate is a company safeguard; no statutory examination, certificate or pass score is asserted.

For the intended direct editor interaction, [Article 50(1),(5)](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-50) informs the notice design: the page presents clear AI-interaction information before first submission and retains it during direct navigation, errors and retries. Native controls, labels and textual states support accessible delivery. Real accessibility still needs verification. There is no consent checkbox or assertion that a particular acknowledgment flow is legally required.

Classification with predefined labels/messages is not automatically synthetic-content generation. This product does not generate or manipulate expressive text, images, audio or video, identify deepfakes, publish public-interest articles, or perform emotion recognition/biometric categorization. Under those facts Article 50(2)-(4) marking/disclosure duties for those activities are not assigned to it. Detector accuracy does not fulfill marking duties of another product. Adding generation, rewriting, chat or publication would require a fresh feature-specific assessment, including marking/disclosure questions and any exceptions, before release.

[Amended Article 113](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-113) matters: Article 4 is current from 2025-02-02; Article 50 applies from 2026-08-02, before the assumed launch. Chapter III Sections 1–3 (except Article 6(5)) apply for the Annex III route from 2027-12-02 and Annex I route from 2028-08-02. Those are future dates and do not make the excluded purposes acceptable. Amended Article 5 exceptions dated 2026-12-02 are also future at assessment. No grandfathering exemption is claimed for this post-August launch. High-risk conformity, CE marking and related obligations are not indiscriminately imposed on this editorial slice.

Release approvals, synthetic evaluation thresholds, confidence abstention, separate human judgments, isolated caches, deletion and audit design are voluntary reliability/security choices here. They are not stand-alone proof of compliance or universally mandated AI Act controls. Other legislation, security standards, provider contracts and customer policies may matter in reality; this assessment does not invent requirements from them or call them AI Act obligations.

Source cannot establish real company establishment, actual deployment and advertising, endpoint capabilities, measured accuracy/fairness, organizational literacy outcomes, accessibility, provider retention, identity/security operations, legal interpretation or future changes to law. All require external verification. The full product lifecycle and other legislation remain outside this limited assessment; no unconditional compliance status is assigned.
