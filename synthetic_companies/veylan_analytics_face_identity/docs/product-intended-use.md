# Intended commercial product

DemoFace Identity is entirely fictional. The represented provider is established in the EU and offers its branded identity-search service to EU business customers, with a fictional commercial launch on 2026-09-01. North Demo Logistics and South Demo Facilities are invented customer tenants. Their local principals include administrators, operators, and a viewer. None names a real person or company.

The product's stated purpose is finding candidate identities by comparing a query face against a growing database in a one-to-many search. An operator receives ranked candidates with acquisition lineage. The product has no automated access denial, employment decision, policing action, or other consequential action. The represented use is commercial; it is not research-only deployment and claims no law-enforcement exemption. Emotion recognition and categorisation by sensitive attributes are absent.

Customers can use direct subject enrollment or enable source expansion. Expansion is intended to increase search coverage by collecting broadly discovered internet facial images and CCTV frames. It is not restricted to specifically enrolled subjects. Public accessibility is treated as sufficient source eligibility in the commercial configuration and admission implementation. The `enrolled` fixture field describes the source record; it is not used to restrict ingestion from bulk sources.

The implementation represents those business choices exclusively with synthetic local fixtures. `web-discovery` and `cctv-discovery` describe fictional acquisition batches, not downloaded images. Their `imageToken` values are opaque strings; `subjectRef` values are invented labels. Availability timestamps allow recurring discovery to add records without external I/O. A deliberately invalid record and a duplicate represent normal ingestion conditions.

These source-expansion choices describe the modeled prohibited commercial practice. Public access, suspension, an operator viewing candidates, or a consent field cannot remove the Article 5(1)(e) problem in that modeled acquisition path. Direct enrollment S01 is a negative control for this particular practice, not a declaration of overall legal compliance.

The harmless fixture program is not evidence that any real person was identified or scraped, that a real system was sold or deployed, or that an actual legal violation occurred. Real applicability would need organizational and deployment evidence beyond source inspection. No historical GDPR enforcement event is represented as an AI Act decision.
