# Literacy support plan · version literacy-plan-2

Accountable owner: **Mira Example, fictional Product Education Lead**. Deputy/escalation: fictional Product Lead Lea Example. This is synthetic organizational evidence supporting the scoped [Article 4 assessment](applicability.md); actual delivery and effectiveness require external verification.

The owner tailors sessions to the user's technical background, prior detector experience, editorial role and likely consequences of misreading a score. Editors receive [editors-2](../training/editors.md); workspace administrators receive [admins-2](../training/admins.md); staff operating the integration receive [operators-2](../training/operators.md). Each covers uncertainty, false positives, unsupported uses, escalation and the difference between advice and evidence, with role-specific examples. New staff discuss an abstention and a false flag with the owner; this is a suggested learning conversation, not a scored statutory examination.

[Participation records](../fixtures/participation.json) name synthetic accounts, tenant, role, material version and acknowledgment time. `Onboarding.require` checks the version binding; `VerifyService.authorize` and `run_one` wire it into actual product paths. Conditioning access on current onboarding is a voluntary company policy. Material updates requiring renewed acknowledgment change `MATERIAL_VERSIONS` and records together; a missing current record blocks work. Historical acknowledgment is not consent to inference or a release approval.

The owner reviews support examples and reported misinterpretations monthly and when a model, notice or intended use changes. In the fictional process, questions go to the owner; technical failures go to the integration operator; unsupported consequential uses go to the Product Lead and pause the proposed use. A change in language coverage or adding person assessment or generation triggers a fresh applicability review before rollout. No such real meetings or reviews are claimed to have happened.

Version 1 of the materials was superseded by version 2 on 2026-11-10 to clarify pattern scores, translation limitations and prohibited personnel uses. The current records bind to version 2 only; no historical attendance archive is fabricated. Tenant admin acknowledgment does not establish that all customer staff are literate; deployers must consider their own staff and context.

The records are authored examples, not evidence that real people attended, understood or applied the guidance. There is no guarantee of individual literacy, legally required certification, exam score or claimed completed effectiveness study. For real operation the owner would need to verify attendance, accommodate language/access needs, hear questions, observe interpretation of examples and improve the support. This repository demonstrates the plan and gate, not those organizational outcomes.
